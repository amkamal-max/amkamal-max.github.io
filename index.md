### Bio
I am an urbanist, architect, and policy researcher whose work integrates geospatial technologies with environmental and social justice frameworks. Currently, I am an Associate Teaching Professor of sustainable Urban Planning at CU Boulder and an Adjunct Professor of Planning Technology at CU Denver. My research advances housing stability, sustainable development, and climate adaptation strategies. She has led multidisciplinary research funded by state and regional regulatory agencies in Texas to inform Low Impact Development policy, and I was commissioned to evaluate the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development’s Neighborhood Stabilization Program. I also authored a widely cited report on sustainable workforce housing in the Eagle Ford Shale region. I also led two international collaborative projects in 2023 with TU Berlin, focusing on geospatial decision-support tools, sustainable development, and real estate economics. My research appears in the Journal of Urban Planning and Development, International Journal of Architectural Research, and Elsevier and Springer book series.
